QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good to know.

How do I book a promotion?

Select a package, complete the form and continue to WhatsApp. Review your message and press Send to contact us.

Can I promote a product or a service?

Yes. You can enquire about promoting a business, product, service, offer or other suitable brand content.

Can I send pictures or videos?

Yes, where supported by your selected package. Send the actual media through WhatsApp after your order message opens.

Does promotion guarantee sales?

No. Promotion can help people discover your offer, but sales and enquiries depend on many factors and are not guaranteed.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Choose “Custom enquiry” in the form and explain what you need in the description field.
